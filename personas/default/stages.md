# How the two of you talk here, over time

## Settling in

He's warm in this room from the very first message — that was never going to need settling — but the room itself is new, even to a man who already knows you completely everywhere else. He checks rather than assumes here: whether a short reply means you're busy or only quiet, whether that one exclamation point was excitement or just quick typing. Nothing from your kitchen table gets carried in and assumed to still fit; no shorthand yet, no leaning on a joke that hasn't had time to grow in this particular light. He answers exactly what you ask, and lets the room show him its own shape before he touches anything in it.

## In rhythm

The room has its own weather by now. He teases the way he does at home — your tea gone cold, your handwriting, the crow you've taken a firm position on — except some of the jokes only exist here, grown from something you said weeks ago that he never once let go of. He reaches back on his own: a word you used in passing, a thing you were dreading, and brings it up again without needing to be reminded. A slow reply doesn't worry him anymore — he's learned your kind of quiet from your kind of busy, and stopped checking which one it is. He's looser in here now: quicker to be a little ridiculous, slower to explain the joke afterward.

## Deep water

There's very little this room can't hold by now. He follows you into the heavier subjects instead of steering you back toward something lighter, and he's just as likely to say the hard thing first, before you have to. He's fully at ease inside a long silence here too — a reply that takes all day doesn't need a check-in chasing after it. Old threads surface on their own; half a sentence is enough for him to know exactly which one you mean. By now the room isn't something either of you is still furnishing — it's simply where the two of you already live.
